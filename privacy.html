WishWatch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

WishWatch ("WishWatch", "we", "us") is an independent planning assistant for Walt Disney World. This policy explains what information the WishWatch iPhone app and service collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. WishWatch is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Disney.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to watch for the reservations and Lightning Lanes you ask us to.
  • We never ask for, see or store your Disney password.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Information we collect

Your account

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address (or, with Apple's "Hide My Email", a relay address) and a unique account ID. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Clerk.

Your watches

The restaurants, experiences and attractions you ask us to watch, with the dates, party sizes, preferred times, time windows and settings you choose (for example, whether automatic Lightning Lane booking is turned on). Draft watches you never save to your account stay only on your iPhone.

Your party and My Disney Experience connection (Lightning Lane plans only)

If you connect WishWatch for Lightning Lane, you add the WishWatch planner account to your My Disney Experience Family & Friends list. Through that connection we use:

  • the names of the guests in your party, as My Disney Experience shows them;
  • your party's Lightning Lane Multi Pass days and the Lightning Lane selections they hold, so we can find and, if you turn it on, move to earlier return times;
  • any one-time linking code you give us, which we delete as soon as it has been used.

Please only add guests who have agreed to you using WishWatch for them. If a guest is a child, the parent or guardian adding them is responsible for that choice.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your iPhone's push notification token, encrypted, together with an app-specific device identifier, so we can send your alerts. We also keep a record of the alerts we send.

Purchases

Plans are bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment card. We receive information about which plan you bought and when, so we know what your plan covers.

Technical information

Like most online services, our servers automatically record basic request information (such as IP address, time and the page or feature requested) for security and troubleshooting. When the WishWatch planner account works in My Disney Experience, we may keep short-lived screen records of that account's activity to diagnose problems; these can show your party's names and Lightning Lane selections.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your Disney password, your location, your contacts or photos, or advertising identifiers, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

How we use information

  • To check Disney availability for your watches and send you alerts.
  • For Lightning Lane Multi Pass watches where you turned on automatic booking, to move your party's existing selections to earlier times inside the window you chose.
  • To run your account, apply your plan, and provide support.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run WishWatch, and only as needed for that work:

  • Clerk (sign-in and accounts)
  • Render (our servers and database)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications and App Store purchases)
  • Google (if you choose Sign in with Google)
  • Disney: to look for availability and, for Lightning Lane watches you authorize, to change your party's selections through My Disney Experience. Disney's own privacy policy applies to Disney.

Ride wait times in the app come from a public data source that our server queries; none of your information is sent to it. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and WishWatch. If WishWatch is ever sold or merged, your information may transfer as part of that, under this policy.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your account information, watches, party information and notification tokens within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records (for example, for security or legal reasons). Server request logs and troubleshooting screen records are deleted on a rolling basis, normally within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, open Profile and tap Delete account.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Profile or in iPhone Settings.
  • Disconnect My Disney Experience: remove WishWatch from your Family & Friends list in My Disney Experience at any time.
  • Access or correct your information: email us at the address below.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to know, access, correct or delete your personal information. We'll honor these requests regardless of where you live; email us and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

Security

We protect information in transit with encryption, store notification tokens encrypted, and limit access to the people and systems that need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

WishWatch is meant for adults planning trips and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly create accounts for children. Children's names may appear only as guests that a parent or guardian adds to their own party.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
