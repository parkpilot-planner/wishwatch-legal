Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email WishWatchSupport@gmail.com. We usually reply within one business day.

Refunds

WishWatch passes are bought through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

I didn't get an alert.

Check that notifications for WishWatch are on (iPhone Settings → Notifications → WishWatch), and that your watch shows "Watching" in the app. Watches need an active pass to be monitored.

How do I connect WishWatch for Lightning Lane?

In the app, open Profile → Connect WishWatch and follow the steps. You'll add WishWatch through Family & Friends in My Disney Experience using a one-time linking code for each guest.

Does WishWatch need my Disney password?

No. WishWatch never asks for, sees or stores your Disney password.

Will WishWatch book things for me?

Only Lightning Lane Multi Pass selections you already hold, and only for watches where you turn on automatic booking. WishWatch moves them to an earlier time inside the window you choose. Dining, Experiences and Single Pass watches send alerts only.

Do passes renew?

No. Every pass is a one-time purchase and never renews automatically.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Delete Account. This removes your data from WishWatch's servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.
